# dliio findings — 2026-06-17 (06042026 Ouster tunnel)

Three findings from a replay/RViz session on the **06042026** Ouster tunnel bag,
in dependency order. Companion to `TUNNEL_FINDINGS.md` (the broader journey) and
`VISUAL_TERM.md` (photometric-term design). Artifacts live in the gitignored
`resple_test_ws/results/dliio_*` dirs.

## 1. VoxelGrid silently zeroed the photometric channel (FIXED)

The new "Photometric Points" diagnostic read **0 for an entire tunnel run** despite
`ouster_tunnel.yaml` configuring the reflectivity photometric term active
(`photometricWeight 0.3`). Root cause: `pcl::VoxelGrid` drops the custom
`reflectivity`/`intensity` point fields during downsampling (its centroid only
averages a fixed set of known fields; `setDownsampleAllData(true)` does **not**
fix it). So the post-voxel `current_scan` — and the submap built from it — carried
an all-zero photometric channel, every spatial-intensity gradient was rejected on
the variance floor, and **both** the photometric GICP term and the COIN-LIO
LiDAR-image term silently contributed nothing whenever `pointcloud/voxelize:true`
(the default).

Evidence (instrumented): `original_scan` post-copy reflectivity max≈75, mean≈11 ✓
→ `current_scan` post-voxel reflectivity **0.000** ✗ → submap gradients valid=0.

**Fix (committed):** in `preprocessPoints()`, after `voxel.filter()`, re-attach the
channels by averaging each input point's reflectivity/intensity into its output
voxel via the saved leaf layout (`setSaveLeafLayout(true)` + `getCentroidIndex`,
O(N), no extra kd-tree). Gated on `photometric_active_ || lidar_image_enabled_` so
the geometry-only path is unchanged. Single-channel sensors handled (absent channel
0→0; the term reads only the resolved channel). After the fix: ~2600/2800 submap
gradients valid, ~3600 photometric points/scan.

**Consequence:** every prior tunnel A/B (gate, clamp, `photometricWeight`) ran on a
**dead term** — those results are meaningless and must be re-derived. The reflectivity
photometric term is also a *translation* constraint; at `0.3` it was ~100× too weak
to register in the gate's eigendecomposition (geometric term is Mahalanobis-weighted,
M≈500/pt under PLANE reg; gate threshold ≈ 0.005·λmax). Boosting it ~1200× made the
along-axis DOF move but oscillate — i.e. it does not fix the *rotational* degeneracy
(see #2).

## 2. IMU↔LiDAR extrinsic: missing 180° yaw (FIXED, validated)

The dominant tunnel failure was **not** the photometric weakness — it was an
extrinsic error. The cloud arrives in `os_lidar`, the IMU in `os_imu`, and per the
bag's `/tf_static` those frames are **180° apart in yaw** (standard Ouster:
`os_sensor→os_lidar` = Rz(180), `os_sensor→os_imu` = identity). But `dlio.yaml`
ships `extrinsics/source: yaml` with **identity** `baselink2imu/R`, so the node
fused them as if aligned. `Rz(180) = diag(−1,−1,1)` flips accel X/Y (motion prior
points backward) and gyro pitch/roll (pitch prior integrates the wrong way); yaw
and gravity-Z are preserved, so startup gravity-align looks healthy and nothing
errors. Telemetry confirms the degeneracy is **rotational/pitch** (Geo Rot Trust
Margin <1 in ~25% of scans, min 0.23; translation observable, <1 only ~1%) — and
the RViz map "fanned" about the lateral axis.

**Fix (validated in RViz, solo):** treat `base_link ≡ os_lidar` and put the real
rotation in `baselink2imu/R`. Rz(180) is symmetric (its own inverse), so the
rotation is correct regardless of the R storage convention; only the small
translation is convention-dependent.

```yaml
extrinsics/source: yaml
extrinsics/baselink2imu/R:   [ -1., 0., 0.,  0., -1., 0.,  0., 0., 1. ]  # Rz(180)
extrinsics/baselink2imu/t:   [ 0.002, 0.010, -0.030 ]   # os_lidar→os_imu (minor)
extrinsics/baselink2lidar/R: [  1., 0., 0.,  0.,  1., 0.,  0., 0., 1. ]
extrinsics/baselink2lidar/t: [ 0., 0., 0. ]
```

(Recorded as the overlay `cfg/examples/ouster_06042026_extrinsics.yaml`.)
`extrinsics/source: tf` is **not** an alternative here — the bag's `os_*` frames
aren't connected to `base_link` in the static tree.

**Result:** tracking went from identity's ~24 m lock (pitch wobble/fan) to **~200 m
of clean, flat, on-axis corridor tracking** (X 0→−204 m, Y ±14, Z ~0) — until #3.

## 3. Localized blow-up on a floor water pool (open, separate issue)

After ~200 m of clean tracking the estimate blew up at **X≈−240 m (pose ~8214)**:
sudden ~5 m jumps, then Y/Z runaway "down and to the left" (Y→−5000, Z→−3000). A
puddle on the tunnel floor is specular to LiDAR:
- grazing beams reflect away → **floor dropout** (lose the dominant pitch/vertical
  constraint in a round conduit; the already-weak pitch DOF then runs away);
- steeper beams produce **mirror returns** → phantom points *below* the surface,
  so GICP pulls the pose **down** to match (the Z-runaway);
- an off-centre pool makes the loss/mirror **asymmetric** → roll + lateral bias
  ("to the left");
- water reflectivity is a viewpoint-dependent glint → breaks brightness-constancy,
  feeding the photometric term garbage exactly where geometry fails.
- the corrupted scan is baked into the submap → positive feedback → full divergence.

This is a **specular-return robustness gap**, distinct from the extrinsic/tunnel
degeneracy. Mitigations to try: reject grazing-incidence / specular returns (the
`intensityIncidence`/`intensityCosMin` infra exists), mirror-return (sub-floor)
filtering, robust loss, lean harder on the (now-trustworthy) IMU prior when the
floor plane drops, and the per-scan correction clamp (`maxCorrTrans`/`maxCorrRot`)
as a runaway bound. Not yet confirmed against the raw cloud at that location.

A literature/OSS survey of these mitigations (with a portability shortlist) is in
`doc/ROBUSTNESS_RESEARCH.md`. Its top recommendation for this failure: a
**ground-plane-prior sub-ground reject** (drop returns below the IMU-gravity-
seeded ground plane — the mirror-return signature; cf. Patchwork++ RNR) for the
"drag-down" mode, plus triggering the IMU-prior lean off the `Geo Trust Margin`
telemetry when the floor constraint collapses (the dropout mode).

The **drag-down half is now implemented** (off by default):
`odom/preprocessing/subFloorReject/*` drops points more than `margin` below a
per-cell, gravity-aligned, dense-bin floor estimate, with a
`Sub-floor Points Rejected` diagnostic. Not yet benched on the pool — the next
step is to enable it (with the extrinsics overlay) and confirm it spikes at
pose ~8214 and bounds the X≈−240 m blow-up without touching the clean 200 m.

The **dropout half is now implemented too** (off by default):
`odom/gicp/adaptiveClamp/*` tightens the IMU-consistency clamp caps
(`maxCorrTrans`/`maxCorrRot`) as the `Geo Trans/Rot Trust Margin` degrades, so a
floor dropout (which collapses the trans/pitch margin once the sub-floor ghosts
are removed) bounds the correction toward the IMU prior on exactly those axes.
The two halves compose: sub-floor reject removes the *false* Z/pitch constraint
→ the margin then reveals the *true* degeneracy → the adaptive clamp leans on the
prior. Both still need benching on the pool (set a base `maxCorr*` cap and enable
`adaptiveClamp` alongside `subFloorReject`).

## Process notes
- Tunnel benches MUST run **solo** — the now-live photometric term adds per-scan
  compute; under parallel/oversubscribed load scans starve and runs diverge.
- The `dliio_ws` image bakes `src/` + `build/`; rebuild it off the committed SHA
  before benching, or an incremental build over a mounted source reuses stale
  artifacts and the baked install overlay shadows fresh `.so`s on `LD_LIBRARY_PATH`.
