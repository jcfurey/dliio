# Findings 2026-06-22 — the camera and LiDAR-image terms are mis-blamed: the camera works-but-starves, the LiDAR-image term is a no-op for the degeneracy

Three parts: **Part 1** — the camera frame-to-frame term is NOT broken
("Visual Points=0" was a measurement artifact); **Part 2** — the COIN-LIO
LiDAR-intensity-image term engages but never rescues the degenerate axis at any
sane weight (the DIV the 2026-06-18 sweep blamed on it is the baseline geometric
collapse); **Part 3** — implemented condition-scaled directional weighting to fix
Part 2; it works mechanically but the n=12 A/B is a clean NEGATIVE (boosting the
aliased reflectivity anchor *tripled* divergence) → the LiDAR-intensity path
closes for this rig; **Part 4** — implemented the camera dense-source fix for
Part 1; it raises population 2.4× but the A/B refines Part 1: the camera term is
FOV-limited (not density-limited), so it doesn't fix the tunnel either. Parts 1–2
reached by instrumenting the mechanism rather than trusting the aggregate verdict.
**Overall verdict** (end of doc): the 06042026 conduit degeneracy is a
missing-observation problem — no available term re-constrains the axis on this rig.

---


Root-cause of the `Visual Points = 0` the 2026-06-18 sweep reported for the
direct camera (frame-to-frame) term on the 06042026 Ouster conduit. Using the
new per-gate instrumentation (`c3c7df9`: `Visual Match dt`, `Visual Rejects
(behind/oob/grad)`) plus a throwaway per-stage split (reference vs moving
projection), the conclusion **reverses** the 2026-06-18 read.

**The term is mechanically correct and engages.** It is not a Phase-2 wiring
bug. `Visual Points = 0` is the *late-run collapse* of an otherwise-working term,
driven entirely by **reference-projection measurement starvation** (narrow,
side-facing camera + a sparse voxelised source cloud in a tunnel) — the same
root cause already documented for the frame-to-**map** camera term.

## What was verified correct (every link in the chain)

- **Image pairing works.** `Visual Match dt ≈ 0.018–0.024 s` every scan, well
  under the `maxTimeDiff = 0.05 s` tolerance. Camera 20 Hz, LiDAR 10 Hz, same
  bag epoch. The pairing-failure hypothesis (`dt = -1`) is rejected.
- **Intrinsics match exactly.** Bag `/lucid_camera_1/camera_info` K =
  `fx 1094.19, fy 1092.23, cx 969.58, cy 721.31`, D =
  `[-0.04409, 0.05337, -0.00124, 1.59e-5, 0]` — **identical** to the config
  overlay. Image is `1936×1464`; principal point well inside it.
- **Extrinsic R is a valid rotation.** `det ≈ 1.0`, `R Rᵀ ≈ I`; optical axis
  `R·[0,0,1] = lidar −Y` (a sideways-facing camera, as designed for this rig).
- **Source frame assumption is correct.** dlio pre-transforms the deskewed scan
  to **world** (`transformPointCloud(..., T_prior · baselink2lidar)`) and aligns
  with `guess = identity` (`T = T_corr · T_prior`), so the visual term's use of
  `input_[i]` as a world point `p_w` (reference projection independent of
  `trans`) is valid. `extrinsics/source: yaml`, `baselink2lidar = I`, and the
  bag's `os_*` frames aren't connected to `base_link`, so TF does **not**
  override the YAML `cam2lidar`.
- **Raw-scan rigid projection is healthy.** Projecting a raw `/ouster/points`
  scan into the camera with only `cam2lidar` + K lands **9364 / 43530 points
  (21.5 %)** inside the image (u∈[478,1464], v∈[0,1239]); the −Y direction the
  camera faces has 10541 returns within 40°. The camera genuinely sees a
  populated wall.
- **The term produces points when the pose is healthy.** Both standalone and
  under the bench harness, the first scans give `N ≈ 2800` source points,
  `pass_ref ≈ 160`, `behind_mov = oob_mov = grad = 0`, **`KEEP ≈ 160` visual
  points/scan** — every gate passing.

## Where the points actually die (whole-run, per-stage split)

`accumulateVisualResidual` runs **once per LM iteration**. Sampling the per-stage
counts across the full 200 s run:

| run progress | `N` (source) | `pass_ref` (= KEEP) | `behind_mov` | `oob_mov` | `grad` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| start (t≈36 s) | ~2800 | ~160 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| mid (call ~480) | ~1690 | ~70 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| mid (call ~600) | ~1470 | ~1 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| late (call ≥720) | ~1100 | 0–3 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

- **The moving (previous-frame) projection and the gradient gate NEVER reject**
  (`behind_mov = oob_mov = grad = 0` for the entire run). Every point that
  survives the *reference* projection is kept. The term's design — previous-frame
  warp, Jacobian sign, gradient cull — is sound.
- **The collapse is entirely in `pass_ref`** (points landing in the *current*
  image). Two compounding drivers:
  1. The registration source cloud **`N` shrinks ~2800 → ~1100** over the run.
  2. The fraction of those points landing in the narrow side-camera FOV falls
     from **~5.7 % to ~0.1 %**. The reference `u_ref/v_ref` ranges blow up to
     **±10⁷** — points grazing the camera z-plane (≈90° off the optical axis),
     i.e. the side wall is increasingly out of FOV / at grazing incidence as the
     trajectory degrades.

## Why 2026-06-18 read "Visual Points = 0"

Two confounds, now controlled:
1. **Whole-run distribution.** The reported "0" was the *modal* value over the
   whole run; the run is dominated by the late-stage collapse. The healthy
   ~160-point opening scans were averaged away.
2. **Run-to-run chaos.** The chaotic basin means one rep can open at `N≈1473,
   KEEP≈3` and another at `N≈2800, KEEP≈160` for the identical config. Single
   reps are not separable; the early `KEEP≈160` is the term's true capability.

## Implication & recommended next step

The camera frame-to-frame term is a **working, measurement-starved** mitigation,
not a bug to fix. Chasing it as "the Phase-2 wiring bug" is misdirected. The
starvation has the same geometric root as the frame-to-map camera term (narrow
side-facing camera + forward motion in a tunnel), which is exactly why
`VISUAL_TERM.md` already recommends the **COIN-LIO LiDAR-intensity image**
(360° FOV, continuous wall coverage) as the better absolute anchor.

**Concrete, testable improvement for the camera term itself:** the term iterates
`input_` — the *voxelised registration* cloud (~1100–2800 pts). It does not need
the voxelised cloud; it only needs world points with brightness to project. The
**dense deskewed cloud** (~43 530 pts, ~21 % → ~9000 candidate pixels) would give
~100× more candidates and keep the FOV populated even as `N` shrinks. This is the
single highest-leverage change to de-starve the camera term, and is independently
verifiable (project dense vs voxelised, count in-image). It is an estimator
change and needs its own A/B + n≥5 validation.

Harness used: rebuilt `dliio_ws:latest` at HEAD (`88f04a6`) to include the
`c3c7df9` instrumentation (the prior image predated it). Sim-time, headless,
container `dliio_demo`.

# Part 2 — the COIN-LIO LiDAR-intensity-image term is a no-op for the tunnel degeneracy at any sane weight

After the camera term, pivoted to the LiDAR reflectivity-image frame-to-map
anchor (`accumulateLidarMapResidual`, `ouster_tunnel_lidarimg.yaml`). Same
methodology: instrument the *mechanism* (the degeneracy-gate rescue) rather than
trust the 2026-06-18 aggregate verdict ("lidar_image injects DIV 2/5"). Traced
`Visual Rescued Axes` (the count of degenerate axes the term actually rescues)
across full runs at two weights.

## The term engages but never rescues the degenerate axis

| run | verdict | `lmpts`/scan | `lmrms` | `Visual Rescued Axes` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| w0.05 rep1 | SLOSH (bounded) | ~600–1500 | ~0.02 | **0 every scan** (1/1615 = 1) |
| w0.05 rep2 | DIV | ~400–1500 (pre-collapse) | ~0.02 | **0 every scan** |
| w0.30 (controlled) | bounded (~45 m) | ~300–1500 | ~0.02 | **1 of 1932 snapshots** |

The term **engages well** — `lmpts ~1000`, `lmrms ~0.02` (good anchor, not
starved like the camera) — but across three full runs at 0.05 and 0.30 it
rescues the degenerate (along-tunnel) axis **essentially never**. It is a
**no-op for the degeneracy at any sane weight.**

## Why: isotropic mass + a λ_max-relative rescue bar

The gate flags the weak axis from the **geometric** Hessian and only lets a
map term drive it if the term stiffens that axis past
`rescue_thresh = degeneracyThreshRatio · λ_max_geo` (= `0.005 · λ_max_geo` for
the tunnel config). If the term does not clear that bar, the gate **erases** the
term's along-axis contribution and holds the IMU prior — so the term's residual,
already accumulated into `H`, does nothing on the axis that matters.

The term accumulates an **isotropic, count-normalised** residual over all visible
points. In a smooth tunnel those are overwhelmingly uniform-reflectivity wall,
which constrain the *already-strong* (lateral/vertical) axes; the few
along-axis-informative points (graffiti/marks) are diluted by count
normalisation. So the term piles information onto strong axes and adds almost
nothing to the weak along-axis — `v_weak·H_term·v_weak` never reaches
`0.005·λ_max_geo`, even at 6× weight. **No scalar weight can move an isotropic
term's information onto the weak axis** — which is exactly why every weight sweep
(2026-06-16, 2026-06-18) failed to separate from baseline.

## The DIV is not the term's fault

Rep2's divergence is a **sudden mid-run geometric collapse** (t≈145 s): `rotM`
crashes to ~0.001, then `deg=6` (all axes degenerate in `H_geo`), `lmpts→0`, and
the IMU prior dead-reckons into runaway (dist 224→6997 m). `deg` is read from the
geometric Hessian, so this is pure geometry, term-passive (`resc=0`); `lmpts→0`
is a *symptom* (runaway pose → map points project out). "lidar_image injects DIV"
(2026-06-18) is misattributed chaos — the DIV is the baseline tunnel collapse,
and the term is a no-op riding along. (Not early-startup: the collapse is mid-run,
on a fully-degenerate tunnel stretch.)

## Recommended fix (estimator change — literature-backed, needs sign-off + n≥12 A/B)

Make the term's information land on the degenerate direction:

1. **Condition-/direction-scaled weighting** (`REVIEW.md #8`): weight the term's
   contribution toward the geometrically-weak axis so it can clear the rescue bar
   without inflating the strong axes — instead of an isotropic scalar weight.
2. **Degeneracy-complementary point selection** (FAST-LIVO / COIN-LIO): select the
   sparse points whose reflectivity gradient is complementary to the degenerate
   direction, rather than accumulating an isotropic strided subset. `REVIEW.md`
   already flags this as the open deviation from those references.
3. **Absolute-floor rescue test**: change the rescue criterion from
   `> ratio·λ_max_geo` to an absolute sensor-noise floor (cf. the probabilistic
   gate #3, which currently only changes the *hold* fraction, not the rescue bar),
   so a genuinely-weak axis can be rescued by modest, correctly-directed mass.

Direct confirmation worth one instrumented run before building: log
`v_weak·H_term·v_weak` vs `0.005·λ_max_geo` at the gate to quantify the gap.

### CONFIRMED (gate instrumentation, w0.05) — the gap is 250×–10,000×

Logged, per scan, the weakest axis of each block: its geometric eigenvalue `λ`,
the rescue bar `thr = 0.005·λ_max`, and the term's own contribution
`term = v·(H_combined − H_geo)·v`, with `supplied = term/(thr−λ)` (the fraction
of the missing mass the term provides; `−1` ⇒ axis already above threshold).

- **The degeneracy on this bag is ROTATION, not translation.** The weakest
  *translation* axis is consistently *above* its threshold (`λ_t ≈ 700–30000`
  vs `thr_t ≈ 600–2000`, `supplied=−1`) — the along-tunnel translation is not
  what the gate holds. The gate fires on a weak **rotation** axis
  (`λ_r ≈ 18000–70000` vs `thr_r ≈ 50000–98000`). (The visible along-X "slosh"
  is downstream of the held rotation / yaw wander, not a flagged translation
  degeneracy.)
- On the degenerate rotation axis the term contributes `term ≈ 2–16` against a
  needed `thr−λ ≈ 1000–80000`, i.e. **`supplied ≈ 0.003%–0.4%`** — a
  **250×–10,000× shortfall.** To clear the bar by weight alone needs
  `w ≈ 50–500`, which would swamp the strong axes (`λ_max_rot ~10⁶`). No scalar
  weight can work — the isotropic mass piles onto the lever-arm² strong axes and
  only ~2–16 reaches the weak one. This is the quantitative proof behind "no-op
  at any sane weight."

Implication for the fix: the term needs its rotation-axis information *selected
or scaled* toward the degenerate direction (options 1/2 above); option 3 alone
(lowering the bar) would let a 0.4%-mass contribution drive rotation, which is
too weak/aliased to trust. Direction-scaled weighting (option 1) on the rotation
block is the most promising.

NOTE: even with the mass correctly directed, reflectivity in a *uniform* tunnel
is itself along-axis aliased — only textured stretches carry real along-axis
information. So the realistic ceiling is "rescue the axis where wall texture
exists," not "make the smooth tunnel fully observable."

Harness note: `ros2 bag play` intermittently hangs post-playback under
`dliio_nrun.sh`, blocking the next rep (rep2 hung 20 min with complete data).
Controlled standalone runs use a `timeout`-wrapped bag play to avoid this.

## Part 3 — condition-scaled weighting IMPLEMENTED + tested; the A/B is a clean NEGATIVE (the path closes)

Implemented option 1 (direction-scaled weighting) as `conditionScaleTerm`
(`nano_gicp.cc`/`.h`) + a default-off branch at the LiDAR-term call site +
`odom/lidar_image/condScale/{enabled,power,cap}` params. It reweights the term's
contribution by `S·H·S`, `S·b` with `S = V·diag(α_k)·Vᵀ` from the **geometric**
eigenbasis, `α_k = clamp((λ_max/λ_k)^power, 1, cap)` — boosting the term along the
weak axis. 7 new gtests + the existing 70 pass (bit-identical when off).

**Mechanism validated.** With cond-scale on (`power=1.0, cap=50`) the term now
rescues the degenerate axis — `Visual Rescued Axes` goes from ~0 to nonzero
(15/1607 in a probe; 3–81 per A/B rep). The boost makes the term clear the rescue
bar, exactly as designed.

**A/B (n=12 each, 06042026, sim-time, in-bag):**

| config | DIV | SLOSH | TRACK |
|---|---|---|---|
| cond-scale **OFF** (w0.05) | 2 | 8 | 2 |
| cond-scale **ON** (power 1.0, cap 50) | **6** | 3 | 3 |

**Cond-scale ON tripled divergence (2→6) while TRACK barely moved (2→3).** And the
smoking gun: per-rep, **more rescuing correlates with divergence** — the ON DIV
reps rescued 24 and 81 snapshots, the ON TRACK rep only 3. So when the boosted
term actually engages the degenerate axis, it drives the pose **wrong**.

**Conclusion — the LiDAR-intensity path closes for this rig.** The directional
mechanism works (it engages the axis); the failure is the *anchor*: reflectivity
in this uniform conduit is along-axis aliased, so a term forced to drive the
degenerate axis drives it incorrectly → more divergence, not less. This is the
caveat in Part 2 borne out: there is no scalar **or** directional weight that
makes the reflectivity anchor reliable here, because the information isn't there.
(The per-scan gate budget still bounds *per-scan* motion, but repeated wrong
rescues accumulate + seed the geometric collapse.)

The `conditionScaleTerm` feature is correct, tested, and **default-off
(bit-identical)** — it remains a valid, literature-backed scaffold for a rig whose
intensity image *does* carry along-axis texture; it just does not rescue this
uniform tunnel. Untested: gentler settings (e.g. cap 20 / power 0.5) — but the
rescue↔divergence correlation makes a "sweet spot" unlikely (less boost → less
engagement → back toward baseline). The genuinely-independent along-axis
observation this tunnel needs is **camera wall-texture** (Part 1: that term works
and is only measurement-starved by the sparse source cloud — the dense-cloud fix
is the open lever), not LiDAR reflectivity.

## Part 4 — camera dense-source IMPLEMENTED + tested; A/B refines Part 1: the camera term is FOV-limited, not density-limited

Part 1 attributed the camera f2f term's collapse to the *sparse voxelised source*
(`input_`). Implemented the fix: `setVisualSource` lets `accumulateVisualResidual`
iterate the **dense deskewed scan** (~43k pts, same deskewed world frame) strided
to `maxPoints`, instead of `input_`; default-off (bit-identical), via
`odom/visual/denseSource/{enabled,maxPoints}`. 3 new gtests + the existing pass.

**Mechanism validated, real-time:** dense on raises the f2f peak population
**2.4×** (max `Visual Points` 466 → 1119) at `Avg Computation Time ~10 ms`,
`starve=0` (4000 strided pts ≈ the LiDAR term's budget).

**A/B (n=12 each, camera term ON, 06042026, sim-time, in-bag):**

| config | DIV | SLOSH | TRACK | f2f Visual Points (median / p90 / max) | comp |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| dense **OFF** (input_) | 4 | 5 | 3 | 0 / 1 / 466 | ~10 ms |
| dense **ON** (max 4000) | 5 | 5 | 2 | 0 / 1 / **1119** | ~10 ms |

**Dense raised the peaks but median and p90 stayed 0/1 for both** — on >90% of
scans the narrow side-camera FOV holds ≤1 source point *regardless of density*
(the reject split on collapsed scans is ~75% behind-camera / ~25% out-of-bounds /
~0 kept). Verdicts are unchanged within basin noise (TRACK 3→2, DIV 4→5).

**Conclusion — corrects Part 1.** The camera f2f term is **FOV-limited, not
source-density-limited** on this rig: density cannot populate an empty FOV. The
dominant limiter is the narrow, side-facing camera + tunnel geometry — the *same*
root cause already documented for the camera frame-to-**map** term in
`VISUAL_TERM.md` ("narrow sideways camera measurement-starved"). The dense-cloud
lever does **not** fix this tunnel.

The `setVisualSource` feature is correct, tested, and **default-off
(bit-identical)** — a valid de-starvation lever for a rig with a forward/wide
camera whose FOV the dense cloud *can* populate; it just does not help this
narrow-side-camera conduit.

## Overall verdict for the 06042026 conduit (Parts 1–4)

Every available auxiliary observation term has now been instrumented and A/B'd,
and **none re-constrains the degenerate tunnel axis on this rig**:
- **camera f2f / f2m** — FOV-limited (narrow side camera; density doesn't help);
- **LiDAR reflectivity (COIN-LIO)** — along-axis aliased (no-op at any weight;
  direction-scaling makes it *worse* by driving the aliased anchor);
- **geometry + gate** — the only bounded config, but it ping-pongs (no along-axis
  observation).

The degeneracy is fundamentally a **missing-observation** problem on this sensor
suite, not a tuning or wiring problem. A real fix needs a sensor/geometry change
(a forward or wider camera that sees along-axis wall texture, or fiducials), not
another weighting of the existing terms. All four added levers are correct,
tested, default-off scaffolds retained for rigs where the observation exists.
